THE AURELIOS ARCHIPELAGO

A vast chain of tropical islands held together not by land bridges but by invisible threads of energy known as Aether Currents — ancient flows that regulate climate, tidal rhythms, and the migration of mythic creatures.

THE THREE TITANS

LEVIATYR — The Sea Colossus. Ancient sovereign of tectonic tides, sleeping beneath the Abyssal Trench.

PYRODRAKON — The Magma Titan. Shaper of continents, locked in volcanic stasis beneath Cindercrag.

TEMPESTARA — The Storm Sovereign. A sky serpent of immense proportion. It is the failsafe of the world itself.
